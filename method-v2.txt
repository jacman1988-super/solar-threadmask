ThreadMask v2 — explanation for the participant

1. Find the solar disk from the image. Pixels brighter than 22% of its 90th
   intensity percentile form a mask; keep the largest connected region and fill
   interior holes. Unlike a fixed circle, this follows an offset disk.
2. Keep pixels more than 24 processing pixels inside that region. This reduces
   edge artifacts, at the cost of losing genuine filaments near the limb.
3. Estimate background with Gaussian smoothing, dividing by smoothed disk
   occupancy. Outside-disk black pixels therefore do not darken the estimate.
4. Measure darkness relative to that background, rather than in raw intensity
   units. Low threshold proposes candidates; a stronger threshold supplies seeds.
5. Close small gaps with one morphological step; keep connected regions with
   seeds and a minimum area. Reject regions larger than 3% of usable disk area.
   Closing can also merge nearby filaments, and large real filaments can be lost.
6. Process at 1024x1024 for CPU efficiency. Enlarge integer instance labels to
   2048x2048 using nearest-neighbor interpolation so labels do not mix.

Parameters: the sigma controls the spatial scale of estimated background; low
controls sensitivity; minimum area controls small-noise rejection. A 24-choice
grid varies those three settings on a preselected development set. All other
choices were set before the new blind set was evaluated. See frozen-config.json.

Historical diagnostic evaluation: for every independent annotator set, compare predicted and true
instances with IoU > 0.5 matching. Average annotators for an observation before
averaging observations. This local implementation has not been certified against
the organizer's scorer. Do not call its number a Kaggle leaderboard score.

Current public-v6 evaluation: organizer_metric.py counts every IoU > .5 pair
and pools TP IoU sums, TP, FP and FN over annotator-image entries before division.
It does not use Hungarian matching or image-averaged PQ. The extracted public
scoring functions agree exactly on our real overlap matrices via a NumPy adapter.
This verifies public-function arithmetic, not execution of Torch or the private
Kaggle scoring service. See results/public-v6/evaluation.json.

The original 135-image holdout informed this redesign and is no longer blind.
The v2 experiment reserves 96 previously uninspected development days (100 images)
and tunes on 32 other days (35 images). Solar structures may persist across days;
a day-group split does not eliminate every kind of temporal dependence.
After v2 results are examined, that set is also no longer blind for later changes.
One prospective observation, 20110109104734Ch.jpeg, had previously been processed
solely to verify sparse RLE pixels against dense instance labels. Its annotations
and prediction quality were not evaluated or used in tuning at that stage.

No neural network, external model, paid inference or test labels are used.
These are established image-processing operations, not a claim of scientific novelty.
Participant understanding, metric parity, final report and submission requirements
must still be addressed before a contest submission.
