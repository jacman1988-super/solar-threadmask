Finding
A full-resolution ridge regressor makes a small improvement over the frozen pixel/component baseline on
reused development data. The combined evaluation gains one annotation-level thin match and removes
three false positives. This does not establish an independent performance gain or a new competition
score.

Method
Ten image features include intensity, local dark contrast and dark-ridge Hessian responses at scales 1, 2
and 4 on original 2048 x 2048 images. An ExtraTrees regressor (48 trees, depth 18, minimum leaf 8)
learns mean foreground votes from 335,163 sampled pixels across 60 training images. Training
oversamples thin foreground; inference uses image pixels only.
The refiner modifies existing instance masks without adding instance IDs. Width 16, probability threshold
0.2 and radius 0 were selected on 19 tuning images. Area/support guards preserve instances with weak
support. The same frozen model and settings were then applied to the additional 40 dates.

Interpretation and limitations
The metric is evaluated separately for each annotation set. Thin matches are annotation-level detections,
not unique physical filaments. The evaluation-only thin proxy uses 2 x area / inner-boundary pixel count at
most 8, with longest bounding-box span at least 40 pixels.
The additional 40 dates were excluded from the current pipeline's training, tuning and first assessment
splits. Older baseline experiments had used the available dataset. These are development results, not a
blind holdout. The additional set adds no thin matches. Descriptive date-bootstrap intervals include zero:
first-assessment PQ gain [-0.001377, 0.004161], additional-set gain [-0.000574, 0.003717].
Reproduction and checks
README.md and REPRODUCE.md describe pinned dependencies, local prediction with separately
supplied weights, and preparing a new training workspace. predict.py checks model hashes, accepts
original-resolution grayscale input and refuses to overwrite output. A reference-image run is compared
against the frozen research label-array digest. The root test suite checks ridge behavior and release
integrity. Full training was not repeated during release preparation.
Evidence and distribution
Frozen protocols, tuning outcomes, failed alternatives and aggregate results are retained under
outputs/solar-research-v5, solar-thin-v6, solar-thin-v7, solar-ridge-v8 and solar-ridge-validation-v9. Root
RELEASE_MANIFEST.json identifies this source-only release; VALIDATION.json records preparation
checks. Earlier manifests describe historical full runs.
