Local research sources consulted on 2026-10-15

Ronneberger, Fischer, Brox. U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image
Segmentation. 2015. https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04597
Basis for the standard encoder/decoder and skip-connection architecture.

scikit-image: Trainable segmentation using local features and random forests.
https://scikit-image.org/docs/0.25.x/auto_examples/segmentation/plot_trainable_segmentation.html
Basis for comparing a supervised multiscale pixel classifier/regressor against
hand thresholding. We implement our own image normalization and feature array.

scikit-learn ExtraTreesRegressor documentation:
https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.ensemble.ExtraTreesRegressor.html

Flat U-Net: An Efficient Ultralightweight Model for Solar Filament Segmentation
in Full-disk H-alpha Images. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07259
Domain evidence for studying lightweight learned segmentation. Our small U-Net
does not implement Flat U-Net or claim its published performance.

Kaggle official Solar Filament Segmentation Challenge 2026 overview:
https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/filament-segmentation-2026/overview
We retain the existing locally audited public-v6 evaluator. No private scorer,
new leaderboard scores, competitor weights or competitor predictions are used.

All inference is local CPU. No cloud training, payment, publication or submission.
Original new implementation: Jacek Adamek, assisted by Codex; Apache-2.0.
Training observations and annotation-derived masks retain their dataset terms.
