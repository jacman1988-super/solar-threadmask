THREADMASK V3

Frozen v2 masks at 1024 processing resolution; sigma 24, low contrast .09,
strong ratio 1.5, min area 60, disk margin 24, closing one. Infer disk from image.
Extract eleven component shape, contrast and interior-distance features. Standardize
using training rows. Logistic mean loss with fixed L2=.01, unpenalized intercept,
5575 per-annotator rows (657 positives). A positive means any annotator GT IoU>.5.
Retain sigmoid scores >= .15, selected from 12 fixed thresholds on tuning data.
No change to retained shapes; output labels preserve instance pixels exactly.

The new split excludes every previously used date. Train 170, tune 55, assessment
106, unused 106. All observing dates stay together. Assessment opened after fitting
and threshold freeze. Public-v6 pooled arithmetic, 165 annotator-image entries,
exact agreement with unchanged pure reference functions through a NumPy adapter.
No Torch/private scorer or leaderboard was run. See JSONs for unrounded numbers.

False positives decline but false negatives rise. Learned retention cannot recover
missed candidates. This is an original implementation of established techniques,
not a claim of a novel segmentation algorithm or competition-winning performance.
